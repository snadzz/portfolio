End-to-End Machine Learning

Outbound Call Center Lead Scoring Engine (ZAR)

Predicting insurance & investment sales conversion across 50,000+ South African VoIP & CRM records using Scikit-Learn and FastAPI.

1. Business Problem & Data Architecture

Outbound call centers waste consultant hours dialing low-intent leads. I merged three fragmented data sources (50k records each)—VoIP Call Logs, ZAR Sales Ledger, and Customer Demographics—standardized mobile prefixes (+27 vs 0), and eliminated post-call data leakage.

2. Model Comparison & Tuning Results

Evaluated four classification algorithms prioritizing Precision to ensure high-scoring leads actually convert:

AlgorithmBaseline AccuracyBaseline PrecisionPost-Tuning Precision
Logistic Regression68.4%65.1%66.0%
K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN)66.2%62.8%64.5%
Decision Tree64.9%61.2%67.8%
Random Forest (Selected)73.1%71.4%76.9%

3. Production Automation & Source Code

Deployed the winning Random Forest pipeline via a FastAPI REST endpoint. When an agent initiates a call, the system scores conversion probability in <15ms and automatically schedules weekly retraining as new VoIP outcomes are logged.
